What to know

Cannabis contains many compounds. Their effects can vary by product and by person.

01

THC

Delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) can cause intoxicating effects and may affect attention, memory, and coordination.

02

CBD

Cannabidiol (CBD) is not intoxicating in the same way as THC, but it can still cause side effects and interact with medicines.

03

Products differ

Products can vary in ingredients, strength, and how they are used. Labels and local regulations also differ.

Philippine legal context

Check the rules first.

Republic Act No. 9165 controls cannabis in the Philippines. A medical reason alone does not automatically authorize a cannabis product or its use. Laws and agency requirements can change, so confirm current guidance with Philippine authorities and a licensed local healthcare professional.

Health note: THC may impair attention, memory, and coordination. Cannabis products can have side effects and interact with medicines. Do not drive after use.
